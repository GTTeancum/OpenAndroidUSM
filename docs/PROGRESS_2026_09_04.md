## Continuation: uninterrupted Level 1 closure

The permanent Level 1 route now completes all 296 steps from the post-crash
street sequence through the final boss and level-end command without a
diagnostic teleport or direct health change. The passing run is retained at
`analysis/generated/post-crash-route-obstacle-recovery-pass14`; its 58
requested captures were checked and then automatically removed while the CSV

This pass corrected several reconstruction and coverage defects from direct
native evidence:

- `CSpiderWebWall::Init` (`0x0031ee04`) builds collision from its authored
  `bbox` child through `createCollisionMeshPhysics` (`0x003d8830`) and assigns
  flags 6 through `setFlagsAll` (`0x003d8cc8`). Room 2 wall 1116 now uses its
  exact 17-vertex, 72-index mesh and removes both rendering and collision after
  the controlling enemy gate clears.
- Normal player movement uses the lower 50 cm sphere of the native capsule
  path. `CapsuleTriangleMesh` (`0x003ce6e4`) only performs its second, upper
  sphere pass while the player is in the wall-contact mode selected by
  `Player::SetNextStateId` (`0x0034b4ac`). The portable collision solver now
  preserves authored face winding and the Room 8 roof-lip/backface route.
- `Player::UpdateMove` state 19 (`0x00350a8c`) still calls `TryGrabPoint`.
  Both authored two-anchor routes now chain while the next point still passes
  `GetBestWebGrabPoint`'s facing/on-screen gates instead of waiting for state
  15 and attempting a stale grab.
- Linked-WayPoint web release follows the velocity construction at
  `0x00350dd2`-`0x00350f94`, including root-displacement subtraction, the
  horizontal 3000 cm/s cap, zero initial Z speed, and motion 28's separately
  retained decaying launch impulse.
- Fresh `CSlider::Update` disassembly proved that state 19 does not carry that
  release magnitude onto a slide. The comparison and literal load at
  `0x0031dd18`-`0x0031dd3a` replace every catch except state 21 with exactly
  800 cm/s. Slide 1039 now advances at that recovered speed; direct and
  uninterrupted route regressions cover the correction.
- The normal-input combat driver now records and recovers from authored
  obstacles deterministically: it tries the native jump control first and
  only uses a normal ranged web press when that jump produces less than 20 cm
  of horizontal progress against the same nearby target. This is harness-only
  logic and does not alter player or enemy gameplay behavior.
