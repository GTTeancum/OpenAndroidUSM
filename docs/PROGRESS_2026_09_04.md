Work resumed from the Level 6 interior-to-Room-1 route, with an approximately
four-hour autonomous work window beginning at 12:20 UTC. This is a progress
record, not a claim that the whole game is decompiled or ready for a full
playthrough. Native function addresses and behavioral details are recorded

  checkpoint 41349 without the earlier premature restore.

  shake/drop sequence, collision updates, effects, sound/camera-shake cues,
  removal, and checkpoint reset are implemented. The normal route plays
  the bridge presentation, returns control, and crosses the first sections.
- Logs expose wall-web state and bridge phases. The autoplay suite records
  build/scenario hashes, timestamps, durations, step counts, and results.
  Missing/stale output, changed inputs, incomplete/empty scripts, and
  failed processes cannot produce a passing suite result.

in Room 5. The 162-step run clears both street gates, completes the facade and
rooftop combat, rescues hostage 60348, catches and rides Slide 198, transfers
